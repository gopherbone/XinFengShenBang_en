# Glossary for Xin Feng Shen Bang (新封神榜) English translation

Setting: a comedic RPG retelling of the classic Investiture of the Gods (封神演義) legend.
Hero 姬平彥 (Ji Pingyan, usually just "Pingyan") is a lazy, wisecracking descendant of King Wu
of Zhou, travelling with his father (姬父, "Pa"), later joined by 殷文 Yin Wen and 紀筱君 Ji Xiaojun.
Tone: light, modern, jokey banter mixed with mythic drama. Keep humor and personality.
